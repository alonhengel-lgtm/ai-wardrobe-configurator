Accessibility statement

We want everyone, including people with disabilities, to be able to design a wardrobe and request a quotation on this site. This statement explains what we did, how to use the accessibility features, what is not yet accessible, and how to reach us.

Last updated: 26 November 2026.

Standard we follow

The site is being adapted to Israeli Standard IS 5568 (based on the W3C Web Content Accessibility Guidelines, WCAG 2.0, level AA), as required by the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 2013.

What we have made accessible

  • Every page can be used with the keyboard alone, and the element in focus is marked with a clear outline.
  • A "Skip to main content" link is the first item on every page.
  • Form fields and icon buttons have text labels that screen readers announce.
  • Status and error messages are announced to screen readers as they appear.
  • Images carry text alternatives, and the 3D preview has a text description.
  • An accessibility menu on every page enlarges text, raises contrast, underlines links, switches to a plain font and stops animations.
  • The 3D preview can be rotated and zoomed with the keyboard.
  • The site respects the operating system's "reduce motion" setting.
  • The design assistant accepts typed requests as well as voice.

How to use the accessibility features

  • Press Tab to move forward and Shift+Tab to move back. Press Enter or Space to activate a button.
  • Press Tab once after a page loads to reach the "Skip to main content" link.
  • Open the accessibility menu with the round button at the bottom-left of every page. Your choices are remembered on this device.
  • In the 3D preview, press Tab until the view is focused, then use the arrow keys to rotate it and + or - to zoom.
  • To enlarge text, use your browser's zoom (Ctrl++, or Cmd++ on a Mac).

Known limitations and alternatives

  • 3D preview: a screen reader cannot describe the picture in detail. Every dimension and finish shown in it is also available in the labelled fields beside it, and the interior layout page lists each component as a button.
  • Voice conversation with the assistant: spoken replies have no live captions. Use the text box in the assistant panel instead; its replies appear as text.
  • Staff area: the internal staff workspace has not yet been fully reviewed.

If any part of the site is hard for you to use, contact us and we will help you complete your design and quotation request by phone or email.
